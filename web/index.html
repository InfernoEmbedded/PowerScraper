Model Comparison Insights
Scenario A: No Battery
Serves as the control baseline. All household loads are supplied directly from grid imports, and solar generation is fully exported.
Scenario B: Baseline (Solar Self-Consumption)
Simple logic charging battery from excess solar and discharging to cover home load. It is price-blind and has no demand-tariff peak shaving.
Scenario C: Smart Heuristic
Charges battery during negative wholesale price intervals or cheap windows before the demand period. Shaves demand imports to 0.
Advanced Strategies
Scenario D: Look-Ahead MPC
An optimal predictive controller utilizing look-ahead foresight. Schedules charging/discharging to minimize total monthly energy cost and peak demand charges.
Scenario E: Adaptive Peak Shaving
Tracks monthly-to-date peak draw and shaves imports dynamically. Reduces unnecessary shallow cycles, maintaining excellent savings with vastly reduced battery wear.
